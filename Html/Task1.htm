Welcome to the Solar System





sun

The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma, and it provides the energy necessary for life on Earth.


Types of planets

  1. Mercury
  2. Venus
  3. Earth
  4. Mars
  5. Jupiter
  6. Saturn
  7. Uranus
  8. Neptune

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest one to the Sun, featuring a rocky surface and extreme temperature shifts.



Venus

The second planet from the Sun; it is the hottest planet in the solar system due to a thick, toxic carbon dioxide atmosphere.

Earth

The third planet from the Sun and the only known astronomical object in the universe to harbor life and liquid water on its surface.

Mars

The fourth planet from the Sun, known as the "Red Planet" due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

Jupiter

The fifth planet and the largest gas giant in the solar system, famous for its Great Red Spot storm.

Saturn

The sixth planet from the Sun, widely recognized for its massive and complex ring system made of ice and rock particles.

Uranus

The seventh planet from the Sun; an ice giant that rotates on its side with a pale blue-green color.

Neptune

The eighth and outermost major planet from the Sun, an icy giant with deep blue winds and extreme cold.
